# IVM Passage Source Note

## Canonical Source for Release 0.1

The IVM escalation requirement is carried into the Atlas from *Causality and
Attraction, Volume V: The Origin of Cosmology*.

Volume V distinguishes three boundaries:

1. the macro-closure horizon, which supplies the enclosing halt condition;
2. the periphery boundary, which is the active edge of the current enclosure
   and is governed by the Isotropic Vector Matrix;
3. the inner boundary, which is the quantized core associated with the zero
   phase.

At the periphery, contracted and expanded configurations accumulate in the
current geometry. When local saturation is reached, Volume V identifies
Jitterbug transformation as the scale-escalation or scale-de-escalation route.
Zero Infinity supplies the scalable reference architecture, while the larger
enclosure continues to project causal structure inward after the passage.

The relevant working-source passages are in
`Volume_V_The_Origin_of_Cosmology_Manuscript_Draft.md`, particularly the Zero
Infinity discussion around lines 530--560 and the three-boundary discussion
around lines 886--902. The published Volume V is identified by DOI
[`10.5281/zenodo.21010141`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21010141).

## Atlas Consequence

Release 0.1 therefore requires every claimed scale passage to record:

- a current IVM address;
- a local Vector Equilibrium;
- an active tetrahedral-octahedral cell;
- a contracted, balanced, expanded, mixed, or unassigned departure geometry;
- the periphery boundary;
- the quantity approaching saturation;
- the capacity condition;
- the Jitterbug transition state;
- the adjacent IVM address;
- what remains accounted for across the passage;
- the causal projection supplied by the new enclosing equilibrium.

The web and print atlas render repeated IVM cells for this reason. The
60-degree lattice is no longer sufficient by itself.

## Evidence Boundary

The Atlas carries Volume V's geometric architecture forward as a required
feature of Nested Causality mapping. The worked candle record demonstrates how
to store and display the architecture; it does not independently establish
that candle extinction is a measured Jitterbug transition or that every
physical scale transition has already been derived from IVM geometry. Such
records must retain their evidence status.
