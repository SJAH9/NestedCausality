# Working Glossary

These short definitions orient readers to terms used across the repository.
They are not substitutes for the formal definitions in the primary works.

**Compactification**  
The treatment of opposing departures within a larger enclosure so their
residual can become bounded without erasing the structure that produced them.

**Departure**  
A change from a reference condition. In the computational models, a departure
may be represented by an action, a change of enclosure, or a residual.

**Departure Calculus**  
The proposed formal treatment of departures, their direction, nesting,
composition, and return relative to defined boundaries.

**Enclosure**  
A bounded causal context within which entities, variables, actions, and
relationships are defined. An enclosure can itself be nested inside larger and
smaller enclosures.

**Final Frontier**  
The stated outer boundary of a model or computation. It makes explicit where a
calculation stops and prevents a finite implementation from being mistaken for
an actually infinite computation.

**Nested Causal Modelling (NCM)**  
A modelling method that examines a problem across nested causal enclosures,
tracks departures between them, audits excluded costs and boundary conditions,
and searches for an intervention that remains lawful across scales.

**Residual**  
The unresolved departure remaining after interaction or compactification. A
model should state how residuals are represented and what threshold matters.

**Scale and depth**  
Related but distinct descriptions of position in a nested system. Depth indexes
movement through enclosures; scale describes the extent or tier at which the
same structure is considered.

**Zero Infinity**  
A scalable reference condition used to describe the point before entities,
games, or departures are defined. Its precise role depends on the primary work
and should not be reduced to the number zero or mathematical infinity alone.
