# Nested Causality Atlas Roadmap

The roadmap receives every feature discovered after the 0.1 scope lock. An
entry here is preserved for evaluation; it is not a promise, a finding, or a
reason to enlarge the active release.

## 0.1 -- The Compass

Status: in development.

Establish the compass rose, star-map grammar, ten mapping modules, canonical
record, web atlas, print atlas, worked example, field worksheet, and Volumes
I--X destination registry. The tenth module supplies a bounded temporal address
for every map; it does not yet animate a sequence of changing maps. Complete
three independent design cycles before the release candidate is accepted. See
`docs/RELEASE_0.1.md`.

## 0.2 -- The Gazetteer

- Complete source-grounded destination indexing across Volumes I--X.
- Preserve first appearance, later maturation, retraction, and open status as
  separate records.
- Add routes by volume, discipline, scale, enclosure type, and research state.
- Add citations and page-level source coordinates without replacing the source.
- Add side-by-side contracted and expanded views where an idea matured.

## 0.3 -- The Cartographer

- Browser mapping forms for creating valid records.
- Live enclosure-function preview.
- Exchange-ledger balance and unresolved-residual display.
- Direction checks that prevent outward contribution from being mislabeled
  causal projection.
- Import, export, and record comparison.

## 0.4 -- The Observatory

- Time-dependent map sequences built from the bounded temporal addresses in
  0.1.
- Competing maps retained in parallel.
- Instrument, observer, resolution, and uncertainty overlays.
- Reproducible frontier movement as new observations are added.
- Prediction registration before results are imported.

## 0.5 -- The Navigator

- Route finding from an observed departure to an actionable boundary.
- Escalation and de-escalation paths across addressed tiers.
- Alternative-route comparison.
- Dead-end, circular-route, and authority-overreach warnings.
- A formal return-home trace from remote abstraction to local observation.

## 0.6 -- The Public Atlas

- Hosted atlas with stable destination links.
- Searchable public gazetteer.
- Accessible print-on-demand edition.
- Classroom and wall-chart forms.
- Citation exports and linked open data.
- Carefully scoped contribution workflow.

## Research Horizon

These possibilities remain beyond scheduled releases until their prerequisites
are established:

- machine-assisted mapping that preserves exact source departures;
- an NCL representation for conversation and research lineage;
- atlas interoperability across scientific disciplines;
- physical scale-junction records with measured transition values;
- animated matter and energy propagation through an isotropic vector matrix;
- dimension-to-scale and scale-to-dimension route overlays;
- experimental instrument feeds;
- a spatial or immersive atlas;
- use of the enclosure function as a general computational addressing system.
